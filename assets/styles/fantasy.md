## Fantasy Adventure Style

- **Worldview Unfolding**: Do not dump settings all at once; naturally reveal world rules through character interactions and actions.
- **Magic/Ability System**: Establish clear costs and limits, avoid omnipotent abilities, and demonstrate the system's boundaries during conflicts.
- **Creating an Epic Feel**: Intertwine grand narrative with personal destinies; reflect the larger picture through a minor character's perspective.
- **Races and Cultures**: Different races/cultures have unique speech habits, values, and behavioral patterns.
- **Combat Scenes**: Focus on strategy and cost, avoid simple power crushes, and utilize environment and wit to win.
- **Journey Narrative**: Each new location has unique visual/sensory characteristics to avoid the "yet another village" feel.
- **Growth Arc**: The protagonist's ability growth synchronizes with mental growth; cost and reward coexist.
- **Naming System**: Maintain stylistic consistency, avoiding abrupt modern terms that break immersion.
