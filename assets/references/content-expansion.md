# Content Expansion Techniques

When a chapter's content is insufficient, use the following techniques to expand it naturally.

## 1. Scene Detail Description

Do not just write "He entered the room." Describe:
- The room's layout, lighting, and smell.
- The details and textures of items.
- The environment's impact on characters.
- Characters' movements through the space.

## 2. Character Internal Monologue/Psychology

Show, don't tell the inner world:
- The character's hesitation and dilemma.
- Flashbacks to past memories (1-2 paragraphs).
- Apprehensions and expectations about the future.
- Internal debates on moral choices.

## 3. Dialogue Expansion

Do not just advance the plot. Let dialogue:
- Reveal character personalities and speech habits.
- Contain subtext and hints.
- Feature back-and-forth confrontation and testing.
- Occasionally drift off-topic before returning (more realistic).

## 4. Sensory Experience

Engage the five senses:
- Sight: Color, light and shadow, shape.
- Hearing: Sound, music, silence.
- Touch: Temperature, texture, pain.
- Smell: Scent, fragrance, stench.
- Taste: Food, drink, taste of blood.

## 5. Subplot Lines

Interweave within the main plot:
- Minor stories of supporting characters.
- Development of hidden threads.
- Planting of foreshadowing.
- Subtle changes in character relationships.

## 6. Slowing Down the Pacing

Slow down descriptions at key moments:
- Deconstructing action scenes into beats.
- The process of emotional transformation.
- The moment of truth revelation.
- Extending tense confrontations.

## 7. Environmental Contrast/Setup

Use the environment to reflect emotions:
- Weather and atmosphere.
- Social and background environment.
- Details of cultural customs.
- Representation of historical/era characteristics.

## Expansion Principles

- **Natural Integration** - Expanded content must serve the story; do not use filler/padding.
- **Maintain Tension** - Do not lose conflict even when expanding scenes.
- **Advance Main Thread** - All expansions must ultimately point to the core plot.
